Fitimo does not give medical advice

The calorie, macro and time values in the App are estimates based on general formulas (shown with the ≈ symbol and as ranges). Individual needs vary.

When you should consult a professional

  • If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or under 18.
  • If you have a chronic condition such as diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease, or a thyroid disorder.
  • If you are taking weight-loss medication (e.g. GLP-1) — your goals should be set together with your doctor.
  • If you have had, or currently have, an eating disorder. Calorie counting is not appropriate for some people; in that case we recommend not using the App.
  • If you experience symptoms such as persistent dizziness, weakness, or menstrual irregularity.

Our safety limits

  • We never set your daily goal below 1200 kcal for women or 1500 kcal for men.
  • We limit weekly weight loss to 1% of body weight, and we state this clearly.
  • We do not give exact dates; time frames are presented as ranges.

Emergency

If you're experiencing a medical emergency, call the emergency number of the country you're in (e.g. 112 in Türkiye and the EU, 911 in the US, 119 in Japan and Korea).
