Information under Article 10 of Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

Where your data is processed

The information you enter in Fitimo (sex, date of birth, height, weight, your goals, health information, food diary and, if you allow it, Apple Health / Health Connect data) is stored and processed only on your phone, by the app. It is not transferred to us (Kubilay Oskan (Fikirizma Media)) or to any third party.

Technical information that leaves the phone

The barcode number and the packaged-product search term, optionally anonymous product information (name and label values), a random installation id for update checks and a random id for subscription verification. These are processed on service providers' servers in Germany, France and the USA and are not meant to identify you. If you email us for support, your contact details are processed only to reply. Details are in the Privacy Policy. If you sign in with Apple or Google, your e-mail address and account id are stored on a server in Germany to provide your account; your diary and health information are not sent to the account.

Your rights

Because your data is on your phone, you can view, change, export and delete it at any time (Profile). For questions under Article 11 KVKK, write to info@fikirizma.com or Kütükçü Mahallesi 2976 Sokak No: 17, Kepez / Antalya, Türkiye; we reply within 30 days.
